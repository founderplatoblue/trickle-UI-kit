Process

From first call to launch.

A typical project runs eight to sixteen weeks. You see working software every week, not a big reveal at the end.

  1. Discover

    1–2 weeks

    Interviews, goals and constraints. You get a written scope and a fixed quote.

  2. Design

    2–4 weeks

    Wireframes, then clickable prototypes you can test with real users.

  3. Build

    4–8 weeks

    Weekly releases to a staging link, with a short demo call each Friday.

  4. Launch & care

    Ongoing

    Go-live, monitoring and a support plan sized to what you actually need.
